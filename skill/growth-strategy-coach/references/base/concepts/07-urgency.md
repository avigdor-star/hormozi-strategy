# Concept 07 · Urgency

## Definition
**Scarcity is a function of quantity; urgency is a function of time.** Urgency limits **when** people can sign up, not how many. A defined **deadline or cut-off** for a purchase or action creates it. Mechanism: it "decreases the action threshold" of a prospect (OF-044). Often used together with scarcity. [stated, OF-054]

## Four forms (the author uses them "ethically" and "without being phony")
[FLAG] Forms 1 and 3 each have two names in the text (Rolling Cohorts / Cohort-Based Rolling Urgency; Promotional or Pricing Urgency / Pricing or Bonus-Based Urgency).

**1. Cohort-based rolling urgency (OF-055)**
- Start clients on a regular cadence; tell prospects the next start date (sign up to begin Monday, or wait). **The less frequent the starts, the stronger the effect** (twice a year: very strong; even every other week nudges). Cohorts also allow choreographed onboarding.
- Late prospect options [stated]: (1) speedy personalized onboarding as a "bonus," or (2) (author's preference) next group starts soon, giving time to review materials and an **extended payment plan** because the start date is far out. [FLAG] "starts in a little" vs "so far out" is not reconciled.
- [claim, unsourced] the biggest sales come in the final 4 hours of the closing day (up to 50–60%), i.e., "last 3% of the time allotted." [derived] 4 ÷ 168 hours = 2.4%, rounded to 3%. The 50–60% has no source.
- [our note] The script of saying a client dropped out and so a slot has opened up is honest only if true.

**2. Rolling seasonal urgency (OF-056)**
- Put **real** countdown dates on pages and copy (the dates must be genuine, or you lose credibility). Rename the same promotion by season/month with its own start and end (New Year ends Jan 30; Valentine's; spring; April). A new ad and page takes "maybe five minutes of editing."
- The author's **number one strategy for local businesses**, which must vary marketing more often. A "new wrapper with a date" gives urgency and novelty.
- [FLAG] The book's own example says "Ends Feb 30!" (does not exist). Use real calendar dates.

**3. Pricing- or bonus-based urgency (OF-057)**
- Make the thing that can be missed the **promotion, discount, or bonus**, not access to the service. For year-round sellers. It would be a lie to say a roofer won't serve you after a date; talk about the **promotion** ending.
- Wording example: say you are unsure how long it will run, since the promotions change roughly every 4 weeks.
- **Price-increase cleanup:** if you really raise prices, announce it (say the price is rising so people should join now); "Never raise your prices without letting people know."

**4. Exploding opportunity (OF-058)**
- For opportunities that **decay with time** (market inefficiency that corrects, early platform advantage, exploding job offer): emphasize the time cost of delay. Examples: eBay→Amazon arbitrage, crypto, stocks, being early on a new ad platform. [our note] Only when the decay is real.

## Urgency in bonuses (OF-062)
- Urgency version [stated]: buy today and I add [bonus], which normally costs $1,000, at no charge. Scarcity versions are not time-constrained. See concept 08.

## Cross-book
- **Leads** (LD-012): people act faster when they have less time, so shorten the window to act: for example a promotion closing Monday at midnight, four hours left on Black Friday, or a bonus available only through Friday. LD-055: add urgency, scarcity and bonuses to the ad CTA. LD-022: the "price goes up every five" schedule adds urgency because prices actually go up. LD-091: the launch "shout" phase cadence (at least twice a day starting 3 days out; every few hours on the day until 2 hours out; every 30 minutes until launch).
- **Money Models** (MM-016): urgency "to enter, to claim, to use," always deadlines (hours; up to five days to use). (MM-039): Rollover Upsell is one-time-only: if you want to be bold, the moment the offer is presented should be the moment to accept it. MM-037 free-gift box [claim, p.109]: "elegant urgency built in + goodwill."
- [FLAG] Ethics guardrail shared with scarcity: reasons and dates must be true (LD-012 says to invent a reason, any reason at all, even a poor one; conflicts with its own ethical-scarcity standard).

## Do not
- Do not give a tool a fake deadline or a recycled "ends tonight" that never ends.
- Do not cite "50–60% of sales in the last 3%" as a benchmark.
