# Cross-reference map · Offers (OF) · Leads (LD) · Money Models (MM)

**Purpose:** one place to see where the three books cover the same idea, extend each other, look alike but differ, or pull against each other. Built from the "Cross-book link" notes in the entries and the "Cross-book" sections of the concept files (framework/concepts/01–25). No new book reading.
**Status:** built by us from our own notes. Not yet independently checked.
**Tag key:** [stated] in the book · [derived] our arithmetic · [FLAG] inconsistency or risk · [our note] our observation.

## How to read the Relationship column
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SAME | Same idea restated in another book. Safe to treat as one idea. |
| EXTENDS | One book goes deeper or applies the idea. Use the deeper one for detail. |
| DIFFERENT | Looks alike, is not. **Do not merge.** |
| TENSION | The books (or a book and itself) give different answers. The user must choose. |
| ONE BOOK | Only one book covers it. Say so; do not borrow from the others. |

## 1. What each book is for [stated unless noted]
- **Offers:** what to sell. Market > Offer > Persuasion (OF-016); build the Grand Slam Offer; enhancers (scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantees, naming).
- **Leads:** how to find the people. Ends at the engaged lead; "closing the sale" is out of scope (LD-003). Core Four plus lead getters.
- **Money Models:** how to get paid more per customer. Attraction, upsell, downsell, continuity (MM-005); the 30-day cash rule (MM-004).
- **Hand-offs between books:**
  - Offers → Money Models: Offers lists "money model / payment terms" as a Grand Slam Offer component and says it is expanded in Money Models (OF-005 note).
  - Leads → Money Models: Leads says Money Models covers what to sell after the lead engages (LD-016 note); the Leads upsell and 30-day examples (LD-061) are the headline version of MM-028 to MM-041.
  - Money Models → Leads: MM-014 points to Leads' launch chapter for the whisper-tease-shout cadence (LD-091).
  - Offers → "Book II": the VIP-service-as-upsell reference (OF-064) does not name the book. We read it as Money Models; the book's own volume numbering is inconsistent (OF-081). [our note]
- **Sales / closing:** no book gives a method. Leads excludes it (LD-003; a "future book"). Offers has only the closing-rate maths (OF-011, OF-033). Money Models has sales-conversation scripts inside two offers (MM-019 Decoy, MM-047 Trial With Penalty). [our note] A tool that needs a sales script must say the books do not provide one.

## 4. Places where the books pull against each other
1. **30-day test** (row 4). Choice required before any calculator. Concept 01 section 4 has the full table and a neutral formula.
2. **Retention assumption vs churn data inside Money Models.** MM-053's 20-month continuity example assumes all 40 customers stay; MM-045's churn figure (10.7% a month for monthly billing) would leave about 10% after 20 months [derived: 0.893^20 ≈ 0.10]. A tool must take retention as an input, not assume it.
3. **Lowering vs raising the promise.** LD-072 lever 2 says lower promises until the close rate drops; Offers says raise dream outcome and likelihood (OF-026, OF-027). Treat as optimization with a close-rate floor; the true promise must stay honest.
4. **Free-offer strength (inside Leads).** LD-042 "stuff they actually pay for" is stronger than LD-004's test "valuable enough that you could charge for it." LD-042's note calls the weaker wording the Offers book's; we found no such wording in the Offers entries.
5. **Scarcity honesty.** Offers sets an honest-scarcity standard (OF-052). LD-012 says "make up a reason… any reason," and MM carries scarcity/urgency mechanics. Guardrail for any tool: reasons, caps and deadlines must be true.
6. **Payments and data.** Card on file, repeated charge retries, ID retention, fees disclosed after the card, 4-week billing, 3% fee, review-for-discount (Money Models); lookalike audiences built from customer lists (LD-051, client data); scraped contact lists (LD-041) and contact details scraped from negative product reviews (MM-039). None of the books discusses platform terms, privacy law or card-network rules. [our note] These are risk areas for any live tool.
