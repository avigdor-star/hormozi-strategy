# Flagged claims · what NOT to use as a benchmark, default, forecast or rule

**Purpose:** one place that collects the authors' track-record numbers, case results, rule-of-thumb numbers with no data behind them, unverified outside sources, and absolute statements. Everything here is tagged [claim] (or carries a [FLAG] about the same number) in the entries (framework/entries/). A calculator or client tool must not hard-code any of it.
**Status:** built by us from our own entries (about 270 [claim] lines read). Not independently checked. Nothing here was re-read from the books.
**Disclaimer in the book itself (MM-069):** the Money Models disclaimer says the author's examples are "exceptional, non-typical results" and "merely illustrative." That applies to every figure in this file.

## 0. How a tool should treat any [claim]
1. Never use it as a default value, a benchmark, a target or a forecast.
2. If shown at all, label it "reported by the author, unverified" and name the book and entry ID.
3. A number the user types from their own business is fine; a number copied from the books is not.
4. A story can show a **mechanism** (how the offer works). It cannot show **how often** it works.
5. If a figure is a rule of thumb the author calls a "pattern" (not a law), say that; the section 3 numbers are the ones most likely to be mistaken for rules.

## 6. Claims that are really ethics or compliance flags
The books present these as tactics; each can mislead customers or break platform, card-network or consumer law. None of the books discusses the rules. [our note]
- **OF-070 wage-payment guarantee:** the author says you will never really be required to hand one over. A guarantee offered as persuasion that is not expected to be honored.
- **LD-012 "make up a reason"** / "any reason (even bad ones)": conflicts with the same section's honest-scarcity standard. A tool must require a true reason, cap or deadline.
- **LD-053 call-outs** (e.g. "class action lawsuit," "government is handing out checks"): treated as misleading by platforms and regulators unless true.
- Also see the payments and data flags in cross-reference-map.md section 4 (card on file, charge retries, fees disclosed after card, 3% fee, 4-week billing, review-for-discount, scraped reviews, lookalike audiences from customer lists); also map row 26 and concept 17 for undisclosed affiliate commissions (MM-067).
- Risk areas for any live tool: ad accounts, client data, payments.

## Do not
- Do not put any figure in this file into a template, default, benchmark table, target or forecast.
- Do not average, add or compare numbers across rows (the two 10.7% figures, OF-024 and MM-045, are unrelated).
- Do not call an author's story "proof." Say "reported by the author."
- Do not drop the [FLAG]s in section 7 when quoting a figure.
